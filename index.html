What we do

Routine maintenance and mechanical repairs, done properly the first time.

  • Log book servicing

    Servicing to your manufacturer’s schedule, recorded in your log book.

  • General servicing

    Oil and filter changes, fluid top-ups and a check over the parts that wear.

  • Engine diagnostics

    Warning light on or running rough? We find the fault before we fix it.

  • Brakes

    Pads, rotors, fluid and brake checks, so you stop the way you should.

  • Transmission

    Servicing and repairs for automatic and manual gearboxes.

  • Air conditioning

    Cabin cooling checks, servicing and repairs before summer hits.

  • Vehicle inspections

    A thorough safety check when you’re buying, selling or heading off on a trip.
