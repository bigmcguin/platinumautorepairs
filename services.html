Our services

Servicing and mechanical repairs for all makes and models. Call us to book, or to ask about a job that isn’t listed here.

Log book servicing

We service your car to the manufacturer’s schedule and stamp your log book. Under Australian Consumer Law you don’t need to go back to the dealer to keep your new car warranty, as long as the service is done to the manufacturer’s specifications.

  • Manufacturer’s service schedule followed
  • Log book stamped and signed
  • Suitable for cars under new car warranty

General servicing

Regular servicing keeps small problems from becoming big ones. If your car doesn’t have a log book, or it’s out of warranty, a general service covers the essentials.

  • Engine oil and filter change
  • Fluid checks and top-ups
  • Tyres, belts, hoses, lights and wipers checked

Engine diagnostics

If a warning light comes on, the engine runs rough or the car just doesn’t feel right, we’ll scan it, test it and tell you what we find before any repair goes ahead.

  • Warning light and fault code diagnosis
  • Rough running, stalling and poor starting
  • Clear explanation of the fault and the fix

Brakes

Brakes are the most important safety system on your car. We inspect, service and repair the whole system.

  • Brake pad and shoe replacement
  • Rotor and drum machining or replacement
  • Brake fluid flush and brake inspections

Transmission

Slipping gears, harsh changes or a clutch that isn’t right? We service and repair automatic and manual transmissions.

  • Transmission fluid servicing
  • Clutch repairs and replacement
  • Diagnosis of gear change problems

Air conditioning

Adelaide summers are hard on air conditioners. We check, service and repair the system so it keeps blowing cold.

  • Performance check and leak testing
  • Air conditioning servicing
  • Repairs to the system and its parts

Vehicle inspections

A thorough check of your car’s condition, whether you’re buying a used car, selling one, or heading off on a long drive.

  • Pre-purchase inspections
  • Safety checks
  • Pre-trip checks
